How this DM works

This first build is an offline procedural DM. It uses D&D 3.5e-inspired checks, encounter tables, NPC reactions, treasure generation and branching narrative templates. It cannot understand every sentence as flexibly as a live AI model, but it accepts free-form actions and maps common intents to game mechanics.

This is a fan-made, 3.5e-inspired companion, not an official Wizards of the Coast product. Rules and content are simplified and should be checked against your books.
